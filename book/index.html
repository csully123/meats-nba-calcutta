The rules

tap to open
Stack
Everyone starts with 200 points. Points are fake; they simulate money. Your total at the end of the regular season, after any bust penalty, is your budget at the playoff auction.
Lines
Real FanDuel prices (another book fills in if FanDuel is missing a market). The number on this page when you place a bet is the official number, even if FanDuel moves after. Prices refresh each morning, around 1 PM, and about an hour before each tip-off window.
Bets
Spreads, totals, moneylines, first-half lines and player props. Add picks to your slip, then mark each one Parlay or Single. The parlay must be from one game (no cross-game parlays); singles can be from any games, and the slip places them all at once.
Alt lines
Tap the arrows beside a spread, total or prop to slide to a different number at FanDuel's price for it (a player over 20 instead of over 10, a team at -2.5 instead of -7.5). Only numbers FanDuel lists are offered. One leg per market: switching numbers replaces, never stacks.
Parlay pricing
Legs multiply at their listed prices. FanDuel discounts same-game parlays for correlated legs; this book does not, so a same-game parlay pays a little more here than there. That is the house rule, same for everyone.
Max bet
No single bet can be more than 25% of your stack at the moment you place it.
Minimum
Wager at least 20 points every week, Monday through Sunday.
Bust
Hit zero with nothing pending and you reload to 100 and keep playing. It costs you at the end of the regular season: one bust forfeits 10% of your final total, two busts 25%, three 45%, four 70%, five 90%. Each bust forfeits at least 20 points regardless, so busting on purpose for the reload never pays.
Foul out
Six busts and you have fouled out, like a sixth foul. You keep betting for pride, but you are automatically last in the auction order behind everyone still standing. If two or more people foul out, the one with the better win rate on settled bets ranks higher among them (wins as the tiebreaker).
Settling
Bets grade overnight from the box score. A leg exactly on the number pushes and drops out of a parlay. A player who does not play voids that leg. A lost leg loses the slip.
Lock
Bets close at tip-off. Until then you can cancel a bet and the stake goes back to your stack.
Who
Tap your name under "Betting as" first. Honor system: bet as yourself only. Other people's open bets stay hidden until tip-off.
