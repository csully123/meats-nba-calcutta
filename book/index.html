The rules

tap to open
Stack
Everyone starts with 200 points. Points are fake; they simulate money. The book runs from opening night through the NBA Finals, and the highest stack after the Finals, after any bust penalty, wins.
Lines
Real FanDuel prices (another book fills in if FanDuel is missing a market). The number on this page when you place a bet is the official number, even if FanDuel moves after. Prices refresh each morning, around 1 PM, and about an hour before each tip-off window.
Bets
Spreads, totals, moneylines, first-half lines and player props. Add picks to your slip, then mark each one Parlay or Single. Parlays are same-game only, one per game; singles can be from any games, and the slip places them all at once.
No conflicts in a parlay
Picks on one game that cannot both win (SAS -15.5 with DAL ML, over 228.5 with under 226.5) or that overlap, where one already implies the other (SAS -15.5 with SAS ML, two overs on the same player), cannot be legs of the same parlay. They are fine as separate singles. Both sides of the exact same market are never allowed together.
Alt lines
Tap the arrows beside a spread, total or prop to slide to a different number at FanDuel's price for it (a player over 20 instead of over 10, a team at -2.5 instead of -7.5). Only numbers FanDuel lists are offered. One leg per market: switching numbers replaces, never stacks.
Parlay pricing
Legs multiply at their listed prices. FanDuel discounts same-game parlays for correlated legs; this book does not, so a same-game parlay pays a little more here than there. That is the house rule, same for everyone.
Max bet
Stakes are whole points, and payouts round to the nearest whole point. No single bet can be more than 25% of your stack at the moment you place it, rounded down. Once your stack, counting open bets, is 20 points or less, the cap lifts and you may go all in.
Minimum
Wager at least 20 points every week, Monday through Sunday, or your whole stack if you have less than 20.
Bust
Hit zero with no bets open and you have busted: you reload to 100 and keep playing. It costs you at the end: one bust forfeits 10% of your final total, two busts 25%, three 45%, four 70%, five 90%. Each bust forfeits at least 20 points regardless, so busting on purpose for the reload never pays.
Foul out
Six busts and you have fouled out, like a sixth foul. No more bets, and you are out of the competition. It does not matter what your stack was or how many you had won; you cannot finish anywhere. If everyone fouls out, nobody wins.
Boost tokens
A boost multiplies the profit on one winning bet. Everyone gets the same tokens, each good for one bet inside its window, use it or lose it: a 1.5x token every week (Monday through Sunday, any game that week); a 1.5x token for the NBA Cup championship game; in the playoffs a 1.5x token for each first-round series, a 2x token for each second-round series, a 2.5x token for each conference finals series and a 3x token for the Finals, each usable only on games in that series. Nothing for the play-in. Pick the boost on the slip when you place the bet; it locks in with the bet. Win and the profit multiplies; lose and the token is gone with the stake. A bet that pushes or voids gives the token back. One token per bet, never two. The max-bet rule still applies, and there is no cap on what a boosted bet can win.
Mega Bonus
One 5x token for the whole season, playoffs included, on any bet you like. When it hits, the book lets you know.
Regular-season prize
Highest stack when the regular season ends gets 50 points and an extra 3x token for the Finals. Second place gets an extra 2x token for the second round.
Settling
Bets grade overnight from the box score. A leg exactly on the number pushes and drops out of a parlay. A player who does not play voids that leg. A lost leg loses the slip.
Lock
Bets close at tip-off. Until then you can cancel a bet and the stake goes back to your stack.
Who
Tap your name under "Betting as" first. Honor system: bet as yourself only. Other people's open bets stay hidden until tip-off.
