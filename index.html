The rules

tap to open
Password
The pool password gets you in. Enter it once and this device stays signed in.
Refresh
Tap the Refresh Button every time you open the page. It pulls live lines and scores from ESPN and reloads everyone's picks. Write-ups do not change when you refresh; they are written once each morning.
Who
Tap your name under "Picking as" before you pick anything. Every pick is saved under that name so the board knows it is you, and this device remembers it next time. Honor system: pick as yourself only.
Hidden picks
Nobody can see anyone else's picks until the game tips off. Before then a game shows nothing about other people's picks at all. Your own picks are always visible to you.
Start
Everyone opens the season at 30 points. The tally runs from there.
Regular pick
+1 if it covers, nothing if it does not.
Lock
Once you have picked a game, tap "This is a Lock" on it. A lock pays +2 if it covers and costs -1 if it does not. Lock as many of your picks as you dare; you can unlock until tip-off.
Mega Bonus
One per person for the whole season. Tap "This is a Mega Bonus" on a pick: +5 if it covers, nothing if it does not. You can move it to another game until the game it is on tips off; after that it is spent. A Mega Bonus cannot also be a lock, and Christmas and Cup games do not double it.
Bonus games
Christmas Day and NBA Cup group-stage games double the payout on a cover (+2 regular, +4 lock). A miss costs the same as always.
Push
0 points when the game lands exactly on the number.
Volume
Up to 5 picks a week, Monday through Sunday. No minimum. Your tile shows how many you have used.
Line
You are graded on the spread shown when you made the pick, not the closing line. Lines appear once books post them, usually the day before.
Lock
Picks lock at tip-off. Until then you can switch sides or take a pick back.
Season
Regular season only, plus every NBA Cup game including the Cup final. No play-in, no playoffs.
Stats
On the Stats tab, tap a name to see that person's numbers: record and win rate overall and by pick type, favorites against underdogs, home against road, streaks, best week and more.
Live vs daily
Live, every time you refresh: spreads, scores, results, standings and everyone's picks and locks. Once a day, each morning: the matchup note, injury list and Claude's pick for that day's games, stamped with the time they were written. A line can move after the write-up; the number on the pick buttons is the one you are graded on.
